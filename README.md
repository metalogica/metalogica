### Hackaton Wins

We've also consistently won at least one hackathon the past 4 years. Here are the highlights:

- 2026, 'Return Of The Agents', San Francisco: This hackathon was different. I shipped [Substrate](https://github.com/metalogica/substrate), an execution harness for agent fleets so that I could deploy an entire web-app, [Roastybara](https://www.roastybara.co/) in under 30 minutes from start to finish. The webapp won the hackathon. 
- 2025, 'AuraSci', Boston, MA: We shipped [Ideosphere](https://ideosphere-app-base-sepolia-git-main-souldbound-labs.vercel.app/) a decentralized prediction market for science. We also got featured in a [Coin Telegraph article for this one](https://www.tradingview.com/news/cointelegraph:c0b7b7329094b:0-gambling-for-science-ideosphere-wants-to-fund-research-with-prediction-markets/).
- 2024, 'Radix Hackathon', London UK: We shipped [Geochain](https://github.com/metalogica/geochain), a dApp built on the Radix blockchain that allows shipping companies to gate escrow payments based on the GPS location of shipments.
- 2023, 'ETH Global' New York City: We shipped [Swirl](https://github.com/swirl-xyz/swirl), a decentralized P2P funding platform for scientific research.
